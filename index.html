Fit Keeper

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version Your workouts stay on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

The exercises you log (name, amount, unit, date and any note you add), your daily goals, and a few app preferences. All of it is saved in a private database on your device, so the app works fully offline.

What the app does not collect

No name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Fit Keeper does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not send your data to any server.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications to show your workout reminders.
  • Vibration for the timer and goal alerts.
  • Run at startup so your reminder is restored after your phone restarts.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Reminders

Workout reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server. They are optional and you can switch them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s notification settings.

Exporting your data

Export creates a file on your device and opens your phone’s share menu. You decide where it goes; the app does not receive or upload it.

Backups

If you use Android’s device backup, Android may include this app’s data in your own Google backup. That is controlled by your phone’s settings, not by this app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any workout inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children’s privacy

Fit Keeper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health note

This app is a simple log and is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise programme.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. The app will never start collecting personal data without telling you first.
